## Deadline, crashes and process cleanup

The supervisor has a wall alarm at the exact campaign deadline and stops active process groups while preserving charges/checkpoints. Each watchdog has its own wall alarm and parent-death handler. Each runner has parent-death SIGKILL and a deadline alarm. The private task-local sitecustomize hook adds the same lifetime protection to every subprocess and clamps its requested address limit to96MiB. It is not installed system-wide. The frozen runner uses sequential children, so the256MiB tree sum applies.

Every guardian reap, including abnormal exits, triggers residual process-group termination and a /proc check for no non-zombie group members before slot reuse. Zombies cannot consume CPU; the direct guardian is waited. A cleanup failure stops progress rather than launching another worker. The parent-death chain and each descendant's own alarm protect against a killed supervisor or watchdog. The hook sets safeguards before exec and checks the parent identity race. The reviewer inspected these paths and tested both owner death and externally SIGKILLed watchdogs with live descendants.
