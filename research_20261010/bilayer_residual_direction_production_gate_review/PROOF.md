# Production gate independent review

## Exact physical normalization
For a translation-invariant scalar density on unordered q-site supports, summing over supports containing a designated origin counts q/N of the total. This follows by summing the translated origin indicator over all N translations; each support has exactly q occupied sites. Nontrivial stabilizers do not change this incidence count. Therefore multiply complete fixed-origin q4 sums by N/4 and q6 sums by N/6 independently. The kernel restricts the support index to a contiguous half-open range, checks range bounds before adding, counts every selected support, and emits only one sector. Assembly proves gapless coverage from0 to the sector total before normalization. GMP integer sums and exact divisibility tests avoid rounding the fractional N/6 factor.

The reviewer ran four fresh CLI invocations with different, interleaved boundaries: q6[0,3),q4[0,11),q6[3,21),q4[11,35). The checkpoint was read from disk by each new process. Independently normalized raw chunks agree with every one of the66 G/H200/H200² entries in the accepted full4×2 Cartesian physical calculation. The44 A/A² entries also agree, verifying the complete coupling polynomial, including all signed commutator and oriented cross terms.

## Binding, coverage and reservation
The configuration binds kernel executable, source/include files, wrapper, geometry, coefficients, dimensions, support totals, affinity, memory and total budget. Each launch rechecks binding before and after child execution. Completed chunks are hash-bound and checked for sector, dimensions, exact range and processed count. Coverage rejects gaps, overlaps and uncommitted reservations. Assembly checks both phases before writing a complete row, whose hash is recorded. An attempt reserves its entire cap before child launch and commits that charge atomically; failures retain the reservation. Each subsequent attempt starts from committed coverage only and receives a fresh charge. Atomic replacement and directory fsync protect against partial writes. Manual ledger edits or a new state do not grant a new campaign budget.

Fresh reviewer rejection checks cover source/binary, geometry, coefficients, configuration, overlap, incomplete reservation, chunk digest, failed-attempt budget exhaustion and final-row digest. The inherited signed128 channel bounds and GMP products are unchanged from the accepted fixture review; range arithmetic checks count<=total-start before addition.

## Corrected launcher acceptance
The separate correction seal677d95d8f1b7c22f7c76db271078c0f4d0376932eacd4374eeb5c39667e627f7 installs PR_SET_PDEATHSIG(SIGKILL) in the child before exec and compares its parent PID after installation, closing the fork/registration race. The kernel inherits the same flock descriptor through pass_fds. Thus abrupt supervisor death requests child termination while the inherited lock prevents a replacement kernel from entering before the old child actually exits. Ordinary timeout handling also kills and waits for the child. Independent adversarial tests exercise SIGKILL, SIGXCPU and SIGTERM, verify the live child's inherited lock inode, check reservation retention, wait for child death, and resume a real one-support4×2 kernel.
