# Alternating-chain neighborhood of the uniform point

This exact producer proof requires independent review. For periodic spin-one H_M(g)=sum_j[h_(2j-1,2j)+g h_(2j,2j+1)], it gives the following bounds for all integer M>=2:

- g in [19999/20001,20001/19999]: gap >= .015 and a unique ground state.
- g in [12499/12501,12501/12499]: gap >= .005 and a unique ground state.

A real symmetric two-spin CELL history kernel supplies the all-size transfer. One-site translation makes the uniform Gibbs staggered expectation vanish. Golden–Thompson and a scalar spectral-measure bound then transfer the fixed physical n32/n64 seed with a quadratic coupling loss. The independently accepted .0248 uniform gap supplies only the short finite Weyl bridge. The proof asserts no perturbed twisted traces, product-transfer positivity, extensive infinite-volume Weyl estimate, new heat campaign, physical contraction or extra trial state.

PROOF.md gives the hypotheses, normalization, input inventory, finite-to-infinite distinction and bottleneck analysis. certificate.json and check_scalar.py contain the exact outward checks.
