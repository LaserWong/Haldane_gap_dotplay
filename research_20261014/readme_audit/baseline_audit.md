## Hamiltonians, normalization and dimensions
- The uniform spin-1 chain has H=sum_i S_i·S_(i+1), periodic boundaries, unit bilinear exchange and S²=2. The full Hilbert dimension is 3^L. L=2 has the periodic parallel-bond convention and exact gap 2. L=3 has exact gap 1.
- The alternating spin-1 chain has H=sum_(j=1)^M [S_(2j−1)·S_(2j)+g S_(2j)·S_(2j+1)], physical size L=2M with M≥2 and full Hilbert dimension 9^M. It has no anisotropy, field or biquadratic term. The shifted Hamiltonian is H'=H+2M I. Dimer excitation energies 0,1,3 have multiplicities 1,3,5. The energy-3 sector consists of quintets.
- The ordinary spin-1/2 bilayer has rung exchange 1, and nearest-neighbor exchanges in the positive x and y directions of both layers are g. There are no crossed bonds. S=σ/2, S²=3/4, 2L² spins, Hilbert dimension 2^(2L²). The shifted Hamiltonian is H'=H+3L²/4 I. Length 2 periodic directions retain parallel bonds. All-size results below refer to square tori, not arbitrary rectangles.
- The fully frustrated bilayer has H'=Q+J sum_edges T_r·T_s. T_r=S_(r,1)+S_(r,2), Q=sum rung-triplet projectors. Each rung-edge contains two straight and two crossed bonds. Perturbation convention is straight J+ε, cross J, equivalent to (J+ε/2)T_r·T_s+(ε/2)D_r·D_s. The convention differs from J±ε.
- The square-lattice large-D model has H=sum_i (S_i^z)^2+g sum_edges S_i·S_j, spin 1 and D=1. The symmetries are U(1) and spin reversal, without full SU(2).
- The AKLT result uses the projector normalization H=sum_i P_(spin 2)(i,i+1). Its bound cannot be copied into unit Heisenberg normalization.
- Complete physical dimension, linear search dimension, retained spectral-state count, sparse support, Hamiltonian-action support, and MPS virtual bond dimension are distinct.
