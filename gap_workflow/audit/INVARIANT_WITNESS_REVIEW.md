# Independent review: rational invariant-subspace multiplicity witness

Reviewed 2026-10-14 20:10 UTC. Production implementation and pinned proof sources were not edited.

## Verdict

No soundness blocker found in `gapflow/invariant_witness.py`. The accepted result establishes the exact ground multiplicity and a gap lower bound for the complete supplied finite definite pencil. It does not discover invariant subspaces from approximate vectors, eliminate full-space inertia, or establish a thermodynamic result.

## Mathematical checks

1. A is checked exactly symmetric; W is checked exactly symmetric and positive definite, including dense metrics. This gives a selfadjoint physical operator similar to W^-1 A. The exact Gram matrix G=V^T W V is checked positive definite, proving full column rank without assuming that V is orthonormal.

2. The identity A V=W V T is checked entrywise in rational arithmetic. Thus the image of V is genuinely invariant for W^-1 A. Also C=V^T A V=G T is symmetric, so T is selfadjoint in the positive Gram metric. T need not be symmetric in its supplied coordinates, and the implementation correctly permits this.

3. Exact Horner evaluation proves p(T)=0. Hence every restricted eigenvalue is a root of the supplied nonzero polynomial. Squarefreeness and one distinct polynomial root in the closed bracket are checked by exact polynomial arithmetic. Additional polynomial roots outside the bracket do not matter; additional roots inside it conservatively prevent acceptance. No characteristic polynomial of either full matrix or restricted matrix is constructed.

4. Restricted inertia proves at least one restricted eigenvalue lies in [a,b), with none below a and no root at b. As p has only one root in that bracket, all r restricted low states have exactly the same energy. Positive Gram guarantees these r vectors correspond to r linearly independent physical states.

5. Full inertia proves no full energy below a, no energy at b, exactly r full energies below b, and exactly r below t. Therefore no low state was missed by the witness: the restricted repeated energy is the full ground energy with multiplicity r. All remaining energies are at least t. Since the ground is below b, t-b is a conservative true gap lower bound. A threshold eigenvalue at t is allowed. If r equals the full dimension, no excited-space gap is certified.

This last completeness step is indispensable. A perfectly invariant exact eigenvector can omit other lower or equal-energy states; the implementation rejects those examples rather than mistaking the witness rank for the ground rank.

## Independent tests

Added `tests/test_invariant_witness_audit.py`, preserving the author's test file. All eight adversarial tests pass:

- A dense SPD metric generated by exact congruence, nonorthogonal witness coordinates, and nonsymmetric T. The witness includes both ground vectors and an outside eigenstate; only its bottom rank is inferred.
- Omitted equal-energy ground state fails full completeness.
- Omitted unequal low state at 10^-100 fails full completeness.
- Dependent columns fail the positive-Gram rank check.
- Lower-root endpoint is accepted; upper-root endpoint is rejected; extra polynomial roots inside the bracket are inconclusive.
- Zero, wrong, or complex annihilators cannot certify.
- Dense indefinite metric and nonsymmetric energy form are rejected.
- A valid result survives monkeypatching SymPy's characteristic-polynomial method to raise, confirming the no-charpoly path.

The independent dense-metric example was constructed from physical diag(0,0,2,5) using A=S^T H S, W=S^T S, V=S^-1 U R, and T=R^-1 diag(0,0,2)R. It is not merely a second invocation of the implementation's matrix construction.

Results: `audit/invariant_witness_adversarial_results.txt`. Combined author and independent tests: 18 passed, logged in `audit/invariant_witness_combined_results.txt`.

## Operational limitations

The input dimension, witness dimension, polynomial degree, and rational input bit-size caps are validated. They do not bound intermediate rational coefficient growth. Time checks are cooperative between phases; a single exact inertia or Sturm operation is not preempted. The output explicitly acknowledges these limitations and requires an external process limit for hard budgets.

The method still receives the full A,W and performs full inertia at three thresholds. It replaces full characteristic-polynomial construction with an already supplied small annihilator/invariance witness, not with an unverified low-rank-only proof. Witness generation and scalable complement certification remain separate work.
